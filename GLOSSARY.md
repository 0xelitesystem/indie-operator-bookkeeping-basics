# Glossary

Plain definitions of the bookkeeping terms used in this reference. Educational only, not tax or financial advice.

**Accrual accounting** Recording income when earned and expenses when incurred, regardless of when cash moves.

**Cash accounting** Recording income when received and expenses when paid.

**Reconciliation** Matching your records against the bank statement so the two agree line for line.

**Owner draw** Money the owner takes out of the business for personal use; not a business expense.

**Gross income** The full amount earned before fees or deductions.

**Net income** What remains after expenses; also used for the amount left after a fee.

**Holdback account** A separate account where you set aside the tax portion of income so it is not spent.

**Estimated payments** Periodic tax payments made through the year by self-employed people in many jurisdictions.

**Category** A grouping for expenses or income that makes the books answerable, such as software, contractors, or fees.

**Commingling** Mixing business and personal funds in one account; avoided because it muddies records and can weaken entity protection.

**Reconcile** The act of performing a reconciliation; bringing records and statement into agreement.

**Close** The monthly routine of bringing the books current and reconciled for the period.
